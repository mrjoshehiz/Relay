# Relay

A mobile-first logistics and delivery app requested from 25 supplied reference screens. Nigeria is the working operating context; Relay is an invented working brand.

Customers should be able to book a parcel, review its price, find shipment updates, access delivery proof, manage a wallet, and reduce form filling with an assistant.

This deliverable is an interactive browser preview. Data persists in localStorage on the current device. There is no real login, courier dispatch, live GPS, payment processor, rider messaging backend, or language-model provider. Every affected workflow must disclose that boundary in its UI. Sample data must not imply a real booking or payment.

Brand assets, production service area, actual rates, staff contacts, payment credentials, dispatch APIs, and AI provider credentials were not supplied. Do not invent them.
